# Services Engineer, Example Retail Cloud

Example Retail Cloud (a fictional company) builds the systems behind its core product and is growing its engineering team.

## What you will do

- Design, build and run backend services.
- Work with product and data teams to ship changes safely.

## What we are looking for

- Containerised deployments with Docker and a CI pipeline.
- Writing onboarding material for new engineers.
- PostgreSQL schema design and query tuning.
- Hands-on experience with Apache Spark for large-scale batch processing.
- Designing and operating GraphQL APIs.

Candidates who do not meet every requirement are still encouraged to apply.
