# Reliability Engineer, Example Media Platform

Example Media Platform (a fictional company) is rebuilding its data pipeline and the services that read from it.

## What you will do

- Design, build and run backend services.
- Work with product and data teams to ship changes safely.

## What we are looking for

- Reducing on-call load with better alerting and retries.
- PostgreSQL schema design and query tuning.
- Go for high-throughput consumers or APIs.
- Hands-on experience with Apache Spark for large-scale batch processing.
- Training and serving models with TensorFlow or PyTorch.

Candidates who do not meet every requirement are still encouraged to apply.
