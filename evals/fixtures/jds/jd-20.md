# Data Engineer, Sample Insurance Tech

Sample Insurance Tech (a fictional company) operates an event-driven backend that must stay available around the clock.

## What you will do

- Design, build and run backend services.
- Work with product and data teams to ship changes safely.

## What we are looking for

- Go for high-throughput consumers or APIs.
- Reducing on-call load with better alerting and retries.
- Production Python services with solid testing habits.
- Production experience writing Rust for performance-critical services.
- Shipping Ruby on Rails applications.

Candidates who do not meet every requirement are still encouraged to apply.
